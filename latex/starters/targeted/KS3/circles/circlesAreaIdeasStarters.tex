    \item $10^2 = \ ?$

  \setcounter{enumi}{6}

  \item A circle has \textbf{radius} $5$ cm. What is the circumference?
  \item The square has side $3$ cm and area $9$ cm$^2$. A circle has radius $1.5$ cm. Is the circle's area more or less than $9$ cm$^2$?
