\section*{Think about Answer Key}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=2mm, topsep=2mm]

\item \textbf{Coventree versus Starlight:} After one year they give the same result. For more than one year, Starlight pays more because interest is compounded.

\item \textbf{Is the biggest percentage always the best?} No. Conditions such as minimum deposits, maximum balances, and time restrictions matter too.

\item \textbf{Why is Trustbridge not a good deal?} It pays only \(2\%+4\%+6\%=12\%\) total simple interest over 3 years, with no compounding.

\item \textbf{Why does nobody choose Nationworth?} The \pounds2 monthly fee is \pounds24 per year, which outweighs the interest unless the balance is over \pounds400.

\item \textbf{Is Halifield paying 2\% twice a year the same as 4\% once a year?} No. \(1.02 \times 1.02 = 1.0404\), so it is equivalent to \(4.04\%\) once a year.

\end{itemize}
