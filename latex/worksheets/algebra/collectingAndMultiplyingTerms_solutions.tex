\section*{13. Problem solving}
Missing terms: 1 \(4b\); 2 \(3y\); 3 \(5x^3\); 4 \(3q\); 5 \(-2x\); 6 \(-3a^2\); 7 \(4m^4n\); 8 \(-6c\).\\
Sam: \(2a+3b\) cannot be simplified to \(5ab\); unlike terms.\\
Priya: not right; \(a^2+a^3\neq a^5\) (only \(a^2\times a^3=a^5\)).\\
Tom: \(12a^2\), not \(12a\).\\
Rectangle: (a) \(10x+4y\); (b) \(10xy\).\\
Area \(12a^2\): e.g. \((3a,4a)\), \((2a,6a)\), \((a,12a)\).\\
Four terms to \(5x-2y\): e.g. \(7x+y-2x-3y\).\\
To \(6a^2b\): e.g. \(2a\times3ab\), \(6a\times ab\), \(3a^2\times2b\).
